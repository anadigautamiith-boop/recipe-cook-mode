Cookalong

Turn a cooking video into a recipe you can follow.

Made for your first time at the stove. We explain the cooking words, say what “done” looks like, and mark anything the video didn’t tell you.

YouTube: we read the whole video, picture and sound. Instagram: we can read the caption. If the recipe is only in the video, paste what it says.
